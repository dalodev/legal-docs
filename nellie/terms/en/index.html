4.1 Subscription dependency and group lifetime

Each group remains active for as long as at least one of its members holds an active premium subscription (the “sponsoring member”). The sponsoring role may pass between different members over time.

  • Participation in a group (viewing it, creating tasks, and marking them complete) is free for all members.
  • Management actions (renaming the group or regenerating its invite code) require an active premium subscription.
  • If at any time no member of the group holds an active premium subscription, a 30-calendar-day grace period begins.
  • If, during that period, any member regains an active premium subscription, the grace period is cancelled automatically and the group remains active.

Important: if the 30-day grace period elapses without any member holding an active premium subscription, the group and all of its shared content (group tasks and completions) will be permanently and irreversibly deleted. This deletion affects only the group; it does not affect the personal tasks each user manages outside of groups.
