Delete your Nellie account

You can delete your Groups account and its associated data directly from the app. Here is how to do it and what happens to your data.

Warning: Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. You will not be able to recover your account or your group data once the deletion is confirmed.

Remember: You only need an account if you use the Groups feature. Your personal tasks are stored locally on your device and are not linked to any account.

How to delete your account

  1. Open the Nellie app on your device.
  2. Go to the Groups tab.
  3. Open the menu on the Groups screen.
  4. Tap the “Delete account” option.
  5. A confirmation dialog will appear, warning you that the action is irreversible.
  6. Confirm the deletion. Your account and its associated data will be removed and you will be signed out.

Data that is deleted

Deleted when you delete the account

  • Authentication account (email address, display name, UID)
  • Group memberships
  • Your user document in the cloud
  • Push notification tokens

Not affected or retained by third parties

  • Local device data: your personal tasks remain on your device under your control; they are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: managed by the Apple App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat according to their retention policies.

Subscription: Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To cancel it, use your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings.

Request by email

If you cannot access your account or prefer to request deletion another way, you can send an email to:

dalodev.dev@gmail.com

Include in your request:

  • The email address you used to sign in to Nellie (Apple or Google).
  • The subject line “Account deletion”.

We will process your request within a maximum of 30 days, in accordance with Article 12(3) GDPR.
