This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data by Nellie (hereinafter, the “App”). Nellie is a visual tracker for sporadic tasks: a semaphore that shows you how long it has been since you last did each task. The App is available on the Google Play Store and the Apple App Store.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully before using the App. By using the Groups feature or creating an account, you accept the processing of your personal data as described here.

This policy is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (General Data Protection Regulation, hereinafter “GDPR”) and applicable national data protection law.

1. Your Tasks Stay on Your Device

Nellie's core principle: your personal task data never leaves your device. You do not need an account to use the App.

All data from the App's core functionality is stored exclusively locally on your device, in a local database:

  • Names and descriptions of your tasks.
  • Categories and their configuration.
  • Completion history (the dates you marked each task as done).
  • App preferences and settings.

This data is not transmitted to any server, is not accessible to the developer or any third party, and is removed when you uninstall the App.

2. Personal Data We Collect

Nellie collects only the data strictly necessary to provide the service, following the principle of data minimization (Art. 5(1)(c) GDPR). Personal data is collected only if you choose to use the optional Groups feature (a subscription feature) or purchase a subscription.

2.1 Account data (Groups feature only)

Using Groups requires signing in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication). We collect:

  • Email address.
  • Display name.
  • Unique user identifier (UID).

2.2 Group data (Groups feature only)

If you participate in groups, the following is stored in the cloud (Cloud Firestore):

  • Names of the groups you create or join.
  • Names of shared tasks and their completions (who completed which task and when).
  • Display names of group members.

This information is visible to the other members of the group — that is the very purpose of the feature.

2.3 Push notifications (Groups feature only)

  • Device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging): used exclusively to send you notifications about activity in your groups.

2.4 Subscription data

Subscriptions are managed through the Apple App Store or Google Play. To validate your subscription, RevenueCat processes:

  • Purchase receipts issued by Apple or Google.
  • An app user identifier (the Firebase UID).

2.5 Data we do NOT collect

Nellie NEVER collects or stores:

  • Credit or debit card data, or any payment data. Billing is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play; no card data ever touches the App.
  • Third-party analytics data: the App contains no analytics services.
  • Advertising data: the App shows no ads.
  • Location, contacts, photos, or any other device data.

Nellie does NOT sell your personal data, does NOT use it for commercial profiling, and does NOT send third-party marketing communications.

6. Recipients and Data Processors

Your personal data is NOT shared with third parties for their own purposes. Only the following data processors are involved (Art. 28 GDPR):

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (group data), and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). Data is processed in Google Cloud, in the European multi-region eur3 (European Union).
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: Purchase receipt validation and subscription status management.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: Process subscription payments as independent controllers, under their own privacy policies.
  • Data shared between users: Your display name, shared tasks, and their completions are visible to the other members of the groups you belong to.
  • Legal obligation: When required by competent judicial or administrative authorities, in accordance with applicable law.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your Groups account at any time directly from the App: Groups → menu → Delete account. Doing so removes:

  • Your authentication account (email address, name, UID).
  • Your group memberships.
  • Your user document in the cloud.
  • Your push notification tokens.

Detailed instructions are available on the Delete your account page. You can also request deletion by email at dalodev.dev@gmail.com.

Note: Deleting your account does not affect the local data on your device (your personal tasks), which remains under your control, and does not automatically cancel your subscription — that must be managed from the Apple App Store or Google Play settings.

10. Security Measures

Nellie implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk (Art. 32 GDPR), including:

  • Local storage by default: most of your data never leaves your device.
  • Encrypted communications with HTTPS/TLS: all communication with cloud services uses secure protocols.
  • Delegated authentication through Apple and Google: Nellie never sees or stores your passwords.
  • Firestore security rules: each user can only access the data of the groups they belong to.
  • Data minimization principle: only strictly necessary data is collected and processed.
  • Google Cloud security infrastructure: we rely on the industry-leading security measures provided by Google Cloud.

Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach, Nellie will notify the competent supervisory authority within a maximum of 72 hours (Art. 33 GDPR). If the breach entails a high risk to users' rights and freedoms, affected users will be notified without undue delay (Art. 34 GDPR).

11. Children

Nellie is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Under Article 8 GDPR, the processing of children's data below the applicable national age threshold requires the consent of their legal guardians.

If you become aware that a child has created a Groups account, please contact us at dalodev.dev@gmail.com so we can proceed with the immediate deletion of the account and its associated data.
